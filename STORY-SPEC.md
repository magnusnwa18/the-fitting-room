# STORY SPEC — *The Fitting Room*
### The knobs. Every field filled. This file defines the story; the METHOD turns it into a book.

---

## 1 · IDENTITY
- **Working title:** The Fitting Room
- **Category / genre:** Literary domestic suspense — unreliable first-person narrator, dual-POV (Daniel / Mara), present-frame braided with past.
- **Comps ("X meets Y"):** *You* (the self-justifying narrator who launders his own monstrousness) meets *Fleabag*-grade interiority and a *Gone Girl* orchestration — but the wife never raises her voice and the gun never goes off. Closest in spirit: a Claire Keegan miniature stretched to a novella, with a Patricia Highsmith engine humming underneath.
- **Logline (ONE sentence):** A self-deceiving architect spends one closing-time hour in a Bath boutique buying his wife an anniversary scarf, narrating himself as the devoted husband — never grasping that the shopgirl is his mistress, that the silk in his hands is a message, and that his wife found out months ago and has spent those months befriending the other woman and quietly authoring the exit he is walking home toward.
- **Audience / shelf:** Readers of literary suspense and book-club fiction who love an unreliable narrator and a reread that re-meanings every line. Promise to keep: the gaps are decodable, the orchestration pays off, and the ending lands on his delusion — not his punishment — so the gut-punch is the reader's, not a courtroom's.

## 2 · ENGINE  *(skill: structure-storyteller)*
- **Central dramatic question:** Not *will the affair be discovered* — it already has been. The question is **what does Clare already know, and what is she orchestrating?** — and, underneath it, the darker one the book actually answers: **will Daniel ever see himself truly?** (No. That refusal is the point.)
- **Theme as ARGUMENT (not topic):** *You cannot be confronted into seeing yourself. The man who has rewritten his own story will rewrite even his ruin — so the people he wronged are freed not by his confession, which will never come, but by ceasing to wait for it and walking out of the story he keeps telling about them.*
- **The conflict engine ("the catch"):** Daniel survives by **narration** — every fact that threatens the devoted-husband story gets relabelled the instant it appears (perfume = a tester; burner phone = for work; receipts = gifts he's been planning; a locked door at closing = customer service). The structural pressure is that **the reader can decode what the narrator cannot**, and the truth is *already loose in the world* — two women hold it — so every laundered line sits on top of a fact that contradicts it. The lie is load-bearing; the book is the slow reveal that the load was removed months ago and the facade is standing on nothing.
- **Movements / acts (5 chapters, because/therefore/but):**
  1. **Closing Time** — Daniel narrates an ordinary anniversary errand; *but* the shopgirl knows too much (his coffee, his wife's "changed size," his fitting room), and a woman was in that afternoon — *therefore* the reader senses the scene is not what he says it is.
  2. **The Royal Crescent** — Daniel lays out the marriage and (laundered) the affair as devotion; *therefore* the reader sees the architecture of his self-deception — *but* Mara's past reveals that the calm woman who befriended her at the shop is his wife.
  3. **Comparing Notes** (midpoint) — the two women's friendship and the comparing of the same lie *therefore* turns the engine: Clare chooses not to confront but to **leave and let him keep his story** — *but* that means the only person who will never know is Daniel.
  4. **The Afternoon** — that very day Clare buys one scarf and they re-tag its twin for Daniel to "find," a goodbye disguised as commerce; *therefore* the frame's lock-the-door moment is recast from seduction to **ceremony**.
  5. **Anniversary** — Daniel carries the wrapped twin home through the crescent rehearsing his speech; *therefore* he reaches an emptied house and an identical scarf on a chair — *but* even then he relabels the absence: *She must have stepped out to surprise me.*

## 3 · VOICE  *(skill: prose-craftsman — sacred; locked before drafting)*
- **POV structure:** Two POVs. **Daniel — first person, present tense** for the boutique frame, sliding into first-person past for his laundered memories (the present-tense certainty is itself a tell). **Mara — close third, past tense** for the months before, converging on the frame night. **Clare is deliberately never a POV** — she is the unseen orchestrator, kept a mystery by being seen only through Daniel's misreadings and Mara's awe. Keeping her off-page is a structural decision, not an omission.
- **Tense:** Daniel present (frame) / past (memory); Mara past throughout.
- **Register / diction:** British, contemporary, Bath-specific (Georgian stone, listed buildings, honey limestone, side-street trade). Daniel: smooth, controlled, euphemism as reflex, the diction of a man who designs facades and trusts surfaces; he relabels in real time. Mara: grounded, sensory, tired-funny, watchful, ashamed and waking; the diction of someone who alters garments for a living and reads bodies and rooms.
- **Rhythm signature:** Daniel — clean, reassuring, periodic sentences that close like a well-hung door, broken by short declaratives he uses as gavel-taps of certainty ("That is the truth of it."). Mara — longer observational runs that snag on a concrete detail, then a short ache.
- **Per-POV voice notes:**
  - **Daniel** — metaphors from **architecture & restoration**: load-bearing, facade, cosmetic cracks, clean lines, plumb, the building "telling you when it's lying." He narrates intimacy as service and suspicion as paranoia he's too generous to indulge. He repeats reassurances *to himself* in the shape of statements about others. He never lies to the reader maliciously — he has already lied to himself, upstream.
  - **Mara** — metaphors from **cloth & alteration**: seams, letting out, taking in, the body a garment remembers, the grain of a fabric, what a hem confesses. She knows things she's ashamed to know (his coffee). She is the reader's bridge to Clare and the only one who carries the full weight without flinching from it.
- **Calibration lines (very-them, per POV):**
  - **Daniel:** *"I'm here to buy my wife an anniversary gift. I want to say that plainly, at the start, because it's the truest thing about tonight — and a crack in a facade is almost always cosmetic; you learn to tell the kind that frightens people from the kind that actually means anything."*
  - **Mara:** *"She knew his coffee before he did, and she'd hated knowing it the way you hate a song you can't stop humming — and a garment remembers every body it's been let out for, so you can read a whole marriage in a seam if you've ever had to take one in."*

## 4 · PROTAGONISTS

› **Daniel Reeve** — 46, conservation architect (restores listed Georgian frontages in Bath).
- **WANT (external, visible):** to give Clare the perfect anniversary — the scarf, the speech, the restored evening — and to keep every compartment of his life sealed and plumb.
- **NEED (internal, hidden):** to stop narrating and actually *see* — himself, Clare, what he's done. (He will refuse this need; the anti-arc is the engine.)
- **WOUND (origin):** raised in a house of unspoken catastrophe — a father whose business and second family collapsed in plain sight while the family maintained a flawless front for the neighbours; Daniel learned that the facade *is* the survival, that what isn't said aloud isn't real, that you keep the front continuous no matter what's rotting behind it.
- **The LIE he believes:** *If the surface holds, the truth doesn't count. A thing unspoken is a thing that didn't happen. I can love my wife and have all of this be true at once, as long as I never say the other sentence.*
- **ARC (lie → truth):** **Anti-arc.** He does not move from lie to truth; offered ruin, he renarrates it as devotion. The arc belongs to the reader, who sees what he won't, and to the women, who stop waiting for him to.
- **Voice signature / anchor:** the refrain "*I'm here to buy my wife an anniversary gift*"; touches the seam of his collar where the scent isn't; calls cracks "cosmetic"; says "*That is the truth of it*" precisely when it isn't.

› **Mara Doyle** — 31, alterations and front-of-house at the boutique (Ferren & Vane, off Margaret's Buildings).
- **WANT (external):** to get through closing, hand over the scarf, and finally leave — the affair, the shop, the town — on the last train; to stop knowing his coffee order.
- **NEED (internal):** to forgive herself enough to be the author of her own exit rather than the woman who waited to be chosen.
- **WOUND:** a pattern of being the convenient secret — kept, never claimed; she mistook being needed in private for being wanted; Daniel's "Clare and I are basically over" was the lie she let herself be altered by.
- **The LIE she believes:** *Being the one he comes to in secret is a kind of being chosen; if I just stay patient and small enough, the front will fall on its own and I'll be what's left standing.*
- **ARC (lie → truth):** from *waiting to be chosen in the dark* → *choosing herself in the daylight* — catalysed, unbearably, by befriending the wife she wronged and discovering Clare is the first person to treat her as a whole person rather than a secret.
- **Voice signature / anchor:** reads people by how clothes sit on them; "lets it out" / "takes it in" as a way of thinking; keeps a tailor's chalk line in her head; the song-she-can't-stop-humming image for things she's ashamed to know.

## 5 · SUPPORTING CAST
- **Clare Reeve** (44) — Daniel's wife; ceramicist who does **kintsugi** (mends broken pottery with gold). The unseen orchestrator. Function in theme: she is the **opposite of Daniel's facade** — she makes the break visible and part of the beauty; she refuses to hide the seam. Mini-arc (seen only obliquely): from the night she found out → choosing clarity over confrontation → leaving, with the gold-seamed bowl and the twin scarf as her only statements. Kept off-page on purpose to preserve the mystery the book runs on.
- **Yusuf Karim** (38) — building-control / conservation officer on Daniel's facade restoration job; the man who keeps flagging the crack Daniel calls cosmetic. Function: external dramatization of the load-bearing lie; the inspection plot that rhymes with the marriage. Mini-arc: professional patience curdling into a formal notice.
- **Priya Ferren** (52) — owner of the boutique, Mara's boss, semi-absent, kind, oblivious; gives Mara the keys and the closing shift. Function: the ordinary world that makes the orchestration possible; the innocent stage manager.
- **Greg** — Daniel's brother, occasional voice on the phone, keeper of the family's old silence about their father; function: shows where Daniel learned the facade, and that he is not the only one maintaining it.
- **Tom** — Mara's younger brother in Bristol, the person on the other end of the train she's catching; function: the daylight life waiting for Mara, proof there is an after.

## 6 · SETTING
- **Place(s):** **Bath**, late autumn. Primary: **Ferren & Vane**, a small women's boutique on a flagged side street off Margaret's Buildings, with a single curtained **fitting room** at the back and a bell on the door. Secondary: the **Royal Crescent** (Daniel's controlling image and the route home), a half-restored Georgian frontage on a job near the **Circus** (his work), the Reeve house in **Lansdown** (the emptied home), the **station** (Mara's exit), a café where Mara learns his coffee.
- **Time period / season span:** Present day. Frame night = the Reeves' wedding **anniversary**, a wet evening in late October, dark by five. Past strands span the previous **six months** (spring discovery → autumn departure).
- **The "two worlds":** the **continuous facade** (Bath's uniform Georgian fronts; Daniel's sealed compartments; the marriage as performed) vs. the **visible seam** (Clare's kintsugi gold, Mara's let-out hems, the crack the officer won't stop circling) — surfaces that hide the break vs. surfaces that honour it.
- **Sensory texture bank:** honey limestone going amber-then-grey as the light fails; rain beading on Bath stone and the smell of wet pavement; the door-bell's two-note shiver; the changing-room curtain's brass rings; tailor's chalk and the warm-dust smell of steamed silk; a flat white going cold; the gold seam catching the lamp on a mended bowl; the dead-air smell of a house with the heating off. *(skill: research-integrator — verify Bath geography, listed-building/conservation-officer procedure, kintsugi terms.)*

## 7 · MOTIF SYSTEM  *(what makes the book feel composed)*
1. **The Royal Crescent facade** — one perfect continuous front; behind it every house is different, some ruined; the lie of the unified surface. *Seed ch.1–2 → payoff ch.5 (the walk home).* MEANS: the survival-facade Daniel was raised into.
2. **The scarf — gold cracks on cream silk** — kintsugi made wearable; the visible mend worn as beauty; the women's chosen message. *Seed ch.1 → payoff ch.5 (its twin on the chair).*
3. **Kintsugi / the gold seam** — Clare's craft; the break made part of the pattern, not hidden — the exact opposite of the facade. *Seed ch.3 → payoff ch.5 (the bowl on the table).*
4. **The coffee order** (flat white he pretends he doesn't sugar) — intimacy Mara is ashamed to hold, which Daniel relabels as service. *Seed ch.1 → recurs → ch.4.*
5. **The fitting room** — the small curtained room; trying on a self; where the affair happened, which Daniel narrates as "where my wife prefers to try things." *Seed ch.1 → payoff ch.4 (the lock).*
6. **"I'm here to buy my wife an anniversary gift"** — the incantation that launders the scene; the refrain. *Throughout → warps into the final line.*
7. **The burner phone "for work"** — the sealed compartment, the thing in the glovebox; it buzzes at the worst moment. *Seed ch.2 → payoff ch.5 (left on the table).*
8. **Perfume — "a tester I sprayed by accident"** — scent as the body's confession; Clare's vs Mara's. *Seed ch.2 → recurs ch.4.*
9. **The cosmetic crack / the failing frontage** — Daniel's restoration job failing inspection while he insists the crack means nothing; the load-bearing lie made literal. *Seed ch.2 → payoff ch.4–5.*
10. **Alteration — "your wife's size has changed"** — Mara's craft; the body changing the garment; the truth altering the fit. *Seed ch.1 → recurs.*
11. **"Stepped out"** — absence relabelled as devotion. *Seed ch.1 (Clare "stepped out" that afternoon) → the final line.*
12. **The lock / closing time** — doors locked: the shop, the compartments, the house. *Seed ch.1 → payoff ch.5.*

## 8 · B-PLOTS / SUBPLOTS  *(length via story, never padding)*
- **Mara's exit** — *who:* Mara. *what:* she packs her life into the last train out of Bath, working her final closing shift; the affair ends not in a scene with Daniel but in her quiet authorship of leaving. *theme-rhyme:* ceasing to wait to be chosen; choosing the daylight. *resolution:* she boards the train, free, having become — through Clare — a whole person rather than a secret.
- **The failing frontage** — *who:* Daniel & officer Yusuf Karim. *what:* a Georgian facade Daniel is restoring has a crack he certifies cosmetic; Karim issues escalating concern toward a formal notice. *theme-rhyme:* the cosmetic lie over the structural truth; a man insisting the surface holds. *resolution:* the notice lands the same week as the anniversary; Daniel relabels even that — proof the anti-arc is total.
- **Clare's kintsugi commission** — *who:* Clare (seen via Mara and via the house). *what:* she is mending a broken bowl with gold; she talks to Mara about the seam; the finished bowl ends on the emptied table. *theme-rhyme:* honour the break, don't hide it — the answer to Daniel's whole life. *resolution:* the gold-seamed bowl is her wordless statement, left where his speech was meant to be given.

## 9 · INFORMATION-STATE SEEDS  *(skill: continuity-keeper — the subtle one)*
- **The affair (Daniel + Mara):** known to both from the start. **Clare learns ~6 months pre-frame** (spring) — the secret she holds without showing.
- **Clare = the wife (to Mara):** Mara meets "a calm woman" at the shop; realizes she is Daniel's wife around ch.2–3; the reader is meant to suspect a half-beat before Mara confirms it.
- **The plan / the leaving:** Clare + Mara hold it; the reader infers across ch.3–4 (never given Clare's full interior); **Daniel never knows.**
- **The two scarves:** Clare buys #1 that afternoon; she and Mara re-tag #2 for Daniel; the reader learns the mechanism in ch.4; Daniel believes he chose it.
- **HARD RULES:** (a) Daniel must never act on knowledge that Clare knows — his innocence is genuine, never performed *for the reader*; the horror is real ignorance, not a wink. (b) Clare's interiority stays off-page; we only ever infer her. (c) Mara cannot say aloud more than she plausibly would on the night; the decode stays in gesture and gap, not exposition. (d) No reveal that Daniel "secretly knew all along" — that would break the whole engine.

- **Chapter count:** 5 long chapters.
- **Word floor per chapter:** **6,000** (hard minimum; reached via the three B-plots, never padding).
- **Word target per chapter / total:** 7,000–8,000 each / **~37,500** total (6–9k band per the brief).
- **Scene marker:** `◆`, every scene tagged with its POV.
- **Working git branch:** `claude/serene-mccarthy-jcwqyx`.
- **Drafting mode:** **compressed** (compress-fiction codec) → `chapters-long/chNN-compressed.md`; expand outside the session with `expand-prompt.md`.
- **Chapter map:** № → title → JOB → movement → POV(s) → B-plots advanced.
  1. **Closing Time** — establish the unreliable narrator, the central image (scarf/fitting room/lock), and the mystery (who was the afternoon woman; why Mara knows so much). *Mov. 1.* POV: Daniel (present) + Mara (past: Clare's first visit). B-plots: Mara's exit (seeded), coffee/alteration motifs.
  2. **The Royal Crescent** — lay the architecture of the self-deception; reveal (to reader) Clare = the woman befriending Mara. *Mov. 2.* POV: Daniel (marriage + laundered affair) + Mara (affair's start; recognizing the wife). B-plots: failing frontage (seeded), perfume/burner.
  3. **Comparing Notes** (midpoint) — the women's friendship and the comparing of the same lie; Clare chooses to leave, not confront; kintsugi paid in. *Mov. 3.* POV: Mara (dominant) + Daniel (present interludes). B-plots: kintsugi commission, Mara's exit.
  4. **The Afternoon** — the day's goodbye, the two scarves and the re-tag; the frame's lock recast as ceremony; the formal notice on the frontage. *Mov. 4.* POV: Mara (the afternoon) + Daniel (present, escalating to the lock). B-plots: all three converge.
  5. **Anniversary** — boutique climax; the walk home through the crescent; the emptied house, the gold bowl, the twin scarf, the final self-deception. *Mov. 5.* POV: Daniel (climax + home) + brief Mara coda (the train). B-plots: all resolve.
