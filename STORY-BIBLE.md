# Story Bible — *The Fitting Room*
### continuity-keeper · the canonical ledger. Update the instant canon changes; flag retcons.

## CHARACTERS

### Daniel Reeve
- **Spelling/shortening:** Daniel (never "Dan" — he dislikes it; Mara/Clare may use "Daniel" pointedly). Surname Reeve.
- **Age/role:** 46. Conservation architect, partner in a small Bath practice; current job = restoring a Georgian frontage near the Circus.
- **Physical anchor:** a man who stands plumb; touches the seam of his shirt collar when uneasy (checking for a scent that is or isn't there); good coat, always buttoned.
- **Voice signature:** present-tense certainty; the refrain "*I'm here to buy my wife an anniversary gift*"; "cosmetic" for any crack; "*That is the truth of it*" deployed exactly when it isn't.
- **Relationships:** husband to Clare (married ~14 years this anniversary — late October); affair with Mara (~10 months, started winter); brother Greg.
- **Wound/lie:** raised to keep a flawless family front over his father's collapse; believes the surface IS survival, the unspoken is unreal. Self-deception is genuine and upstream — he is not performing innocence for the reader.
- **What he knows & when:** knows about the affair (his own). **Never learns** Clare knows, that the women are friends, that the scarf is a message, or that Clare is leaving. Believes throughout he is buying a surprise gift and that the house is intact.
- **Status over timeline:** stable surface, sealed compartments; on the frame night, serene and rehearsing; ends alone, delusion intact.

### Mara Doyle
- **Spelling/shortening:** Mara (not Mary). Surname Doyle. Irish family, raised in Bristol.
- **Age/role:** 31. Alterations + front-of-house at Ferren & Vane. Skilled tailor; the one who took in/let out Clare's clothes (and noticed).
- **Physical anchor:** tailor's chalk dust on her fingers; tucks a tape measure habit; reads people by how a garment sits.
- **Voice signature:** "lets it out / takes it in" as a way of thinking; the song-she-can't-stop-humming image for shameful knowledge; tired-funny, watchful.
- **Relationships:** affair with Daniel; friendship with Clare (built spring→autumn at the shop); boss Priya; brother Tom in Bristol.
- **Wound/lie:** the convenient secret, kept never claimed; believes being chosen in the dark is being chosen.
- **What she knows & when:** knows the affair from the start; **realizes "the calm woman" is Daniel's wife in late spring/early summer** (ch.2–3 territory); becomes Clare's confidante; co-authors the two-scarf message (ch.4). Knows Clare is leaving. Does NOT know every detail of Clare's interior plan — she's a partner, not a puppeteer.
- **Status over timeline:** waking, ashamed, then resolved; on the frame night works her last closing shift and leaves on the last train.

### Clare Reeve  *(unseen orchestrator — NEVER a POV)*
- **Spelling:** Clare (no "i"). Surname Reeve.
- **Age/role:** 44. Ceramicist; specializes in **kintsugi** (gold-lacquer repair). Quiet, exact, kind, unflinching.
- **Physical anchor:** gold under her nails from the lacquer work; very still; mends in lamplight.
- **Voice (heard only via others):** spare, warm, devastating in its calm; never raises her voice.
- **Relationships:** wife to Daniel; improbable friend to Mara.
- **What she knows & when:** **discovers the affair in spring** (~6 months pre-frame). Chooses not to confront. Befriends Mara. Decides to leave. Buys scarf #1 and arranges the re-tag of #2 on the afternoon of the anniversary. Leaves the house with the gold bowl and the twin scarf as her only statements.
- **Continuity rule:** her interiority is never rendered directly. We infer her only through Mara's awe, Daniel's misreadings, and the artifacts (bowl, scarf, the returned/left burner). Keep her a mystery to the very end.

### Yusuf Karim
- 38. Bath building-control / conservation officer on Daniel's frontage job. Professional, patient, increasingly firm. Issues escalating concern → a formal notice the week of the anniversary. Function: the load-bearing lie made literal.

### Priya Ferren
- 52. Owner of Ferren & Vane. Kind, semi-absent, oblivious to the affair and the orchestration. Hands Mara the keys for the closing shift. The innocent stage manager.

### Greg Reeve
- Daniel's older brother. Phone voice. Keeper of the family's old silence about their father's collapse. Confirms the source of Daniel's facade-instinct.

### Tom Doyle
- Mara's younger brother, Bristol. The waiting daylight life; the person meeting the last train.

## PLACES & GEOGRAPHY
- **Ferren & Vane** — small women's boutique on a flagged side street off **Margaret's Buildings**, Bath. Door bell (two-note). Single curtained **fitting room** at the back, brass curtain rings. Counter, a back room for alterations, a stockroom. ~12 min walk uphill from here to the Reeve house in Lansdown.
- **The Royal Crescent** — Daniel's controlling image and part of his route home; one continuous Georgian facade, 30 houses behind it all different. Walk-by on the way up to Lansdown.
- **The frontage job** — a listed Georgian terrace house near **the Circus**, scaffolded; the cracked ashlar Daniel calls cosmetic.
- **The Reeve house** — Lansdown, Bath; Clare's studio/kiln in a converted outbuilding; the kitchen table where the bowl ends up.
- **Bath Spa station** — Mara's exit; last train to Bristol.
- **Travel/time:** boutique → Lansdown house ~12–15 min uphill on foot, passing the Crescent. Bath → Bristol ~15 min by train.

## TIMELINE (relative)
- **~14 yrs before frame:** Daniel & Clare marry (late October).
- **~10 months before frame (winter):** affair with Mara begins.
- **~6 months before (spring):** Clare discovers the affair. Does not confront. Begins coming to the boutique.
- **Spring→summer:** Clare befriends Mara; Mara realizes who she is; the comparing of notes; the friendship.
- **Late summer→autumn:** the decision to leave; the plan; Clare's kintsugi commission underway; Karim's concern on the frontage escalates.
- **Frame day (anniversary, late October, wet):** afternoon — Clare visits the shop, buys scarf #1, they re-tag #2, goodbye. Karim's formal notice lands this week. Evening (~5pm, dark) — Daniel arrives near closing; Mara locks up; the scarf; he walks home; the empty house; the final line. Same night — Mara catches the last train.

## INFORMATION STATE — "who knows what, when"  *(the subtle one)*
- **Secret: the affair.** Known to: Daniel & Mara (start, winter). Revealed to Clare: spring (she tells no one she knows). Revealed to reader: decoded across ch.1–2. **Never revealed to Daniel that Clare knows.**
- **Secret: Clare = the wife (from Mara's side).** Mara learns: late spring/early summer. Reader: suspects half a beat before Mara confirms (ch.2–3).
- **Secret: the plan / Clare leaving.** Known to: Clare & (in part) Mara. Reader: infers ch.3–4. **Daniel: never.**
- **Secret: the two scarves / the re-tag.** Known to: Clare & Mara (frame afternoon). Reader: learns ch.4. Daniel: believes he chose it.
- **HARD RULES:** (1) Daniel never acts on knowledge that Clare knows — innocence genuine, never a wink to the reader. (2) Clare's interior never rendered directly. (3) Mara never says aloud more than she plausibly would; decode lives in gesture/gap. (4) No "he knew all along" reveal — it would break the engine.

## VOICE / STYLE RULES
- **POV:** Daniel — first person, **present** (frame) / past (laundered memory). Mara — **close third, past**. Clare — never POV.
- **Tense discipline:** Daniel's present-tense certainty is a tell; don't let him slip into reflective past about the affair without relabelling. Mara's past is warm and exact.
- **Register:** British, Bath-specific. Daniel: architecture/restoration metaphors, euphemism, periodic sentences closing like a door, short gavel-tap declaratives. Mara: cloth/alteration metaphors, longer observational runs snagging on a detail, then a short ache.
- **Calibration:**
  - Daniel: *"I'm here to buy my wife an anniversary gift. I want to say that plainly, because it's the truest thing about tonight — and a crack in a facade is almost always cosmetic."*
  - Mara: *"She knew his coffee before he did, and she'd hated knowing it the way you hate a song you can't stop humming."*
- **Scene marker:** `◆`, each tagged with POV.
